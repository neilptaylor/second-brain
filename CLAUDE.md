# Working in this repo

This is Neil Taylor's second brain for Me & My Old Man. It is the single source of truth for how the business thinks, writes, sells and delivers.

Read `README.md` first — it is the identity doc. Then `INDEX.md` for what lives where.

## Non-negotiables for anything written here or from here

- No em dashes. Anywhere. Including plain chat replies.
- Double quotes for speech and quoted text, never single.
- "Audio documentaries", never films, videos or movies.
- No "nothing salesy" style disclaimers. The disclaimer is itself salesy.
- Never name sales gurus or frameworks in client-facing drafts.
- Check `knowledge/voice/VOICE_PROFILE_Neil_Taylor.md` before writing in Neil's voice.
- Check `knowledge/offer/pricing.md` before quoting any price. The pricing in `README.md` is out of date.

## Layout

- `README.md` — who Neil is, what the business is, core beliefs, voice
- `knowledge/` — durable facts: voice profile, voice of customer, offer and pricing
- `skills/` — the reusable Claude skills that do the work
- `workflows/scheduled-tasks/` — the automations that run on a schedule
- `prompts/` — one-off prompt templates
- `docs/` — notes about the brain itself
- `scratch/` — working files, not a source of truth
