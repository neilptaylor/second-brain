## LIVE & ACTIVE

### 1. "10 Questions To Ask Your Parents (Before It's Too Late)"
**Format:** PDF Guide (full implementation guide with scripts)
**Status:** Live
**Angle:** Practical question framework + word-for-word conversation scripts
**What it does well:**
- Comprehensive. Opening scripts, objection handling, where to have the conversations, how to record.
- The "Handling Common Objections" section is genuinely useful and smart.
- Good use of "what to do when emotions run high."
- CTA lands on Discovery Call.

**What it doesn't do (opportunity gaps):**
- Tonally more instructional than emotional. Reads like a guide, not like Neil.
- Leans into "before it's too late" framing — which the updated brand guidelines now flag as risky standalone. No transformation/possibility paired with the urgency.
- No sibling angle anywhere (big miss per the Feb 2026 Brand Guidelines update).
- The connection/drift insight is buried. "Nobody stopped loving each other" doesn't appear.
- Design/formatting unknown (have the .docx, not the designed PDF).

**Verdict when next creating a question-based lead magnet:** Differentiate from this. Go deeper on the emotional reconnection angle, lighter on the practical scripts. Or update this one to add sibling framing and the new brand positioning.

## MISSING (Opportunities Not Yet Built)

Based on the existing inventory, these angles haven't been covered:

1. **The Sibling Reconnection angle** — No lead magnet currently leads with "Your siblings will thank you." This is the biggest surprise benefit in the VoC data and currently zero lead magnets target it.
2. **The Drift framing** — No asset named around "drift" or the reconnection-not-preservation positioning.
3. **The "Closed Book Dad" archetype** — A lead magnet specifically for daughters/sons of quiet, story-performing-but-self-concealing fathers.
4. **A 5-day email mini-series** — No email-native lead magnet. All assets are download-first.
5. **Parent-facing** — Everything is child-facing. Nothing asks the parent: *"What if someone finally asked you the right questions?"*

## Steve Wade Reference
Folder: `Projects/Lead Magnets/Steve Wade Lead Magnet Approach/`

**Context (critical):** Steve Wade's business is B2B and tech-focused. Neil's is B2C, emotion-led, life stories, built entirely on trust. The models and approaches are structurally transferable but the tone, hooks, and positioning are completely different. When reading Steve Wade's materials: extract the *structural logic* (funnel architecture, format choices, sequencing) — never the copy or the emotional register. Do not let his style bleed into MMOM assets.

When files appear in this folder, read them for: lead magnet architecture patterns, funnel sequencing, conversion mechanics. Ignore: specific language, B2B framing, tech/feature-led angles.
