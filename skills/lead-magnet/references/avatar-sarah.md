## The Positioning Shift (Critical — Read This First)

**OLD framing (wrong):** Sarah is desperately trying to capture her dad's stories before dementia makes it impossible.

**NEW framing (correct):** Sarah is the **Emotional CEO of a family that has naturally drifted apart**, carrying the invisible burden of keeping everyone connected while life pulls them in different directions.

The real problem, in Brittany's words:
> "We were a very tight knit family growing up... and now we're busy, we've got our own things going on."

What she thinks she's buying: *Recording Dad's stories before dementia makes it impossible.*
What she's actually buying: *"Bringing your original family back together after the natural pull-apart of adult life."*

---

## Demographic Snapshot

- **Age:** 35-50 (sweet spot: 40-46)
- **Life stage:** Sandwich Generation — caring for kids AND aging parents simultaneously
- **Gender:** 80% female (the "Emotional CEO" role defaults to daughters)
- **Geography:** 200+ miles from parents (40%), OR lives locally but feels emotionally distant (60%). Often international.
- **Family structure:** 2-3 siblings (she takes the lead), 1-3 own children aged 5-18, parents 65-80 (showing signs of aging, not in crisis yet)
- **Career:** Full-time professional/corporate, often remote. Income $75K-$150K household. Has resources, no time.

## How to Speak to Sarah

**DO:**
- Lead with connection, not death
- Emphasise the sibling surprise element
- Use verbatim customer quotes
- Show the process, not just the product
- Frame as "re-centering" not "preserving"

**DON'T:**
- Make it sound morbid or death-focused
- Use banned legacy/treasure/heirloom language
- Suggest she should DIY it
- Make her feel more guilty
- Focus only on the audiobook product

## The Sarah Underline Test

A line passes this test if Sarah pauses, re-reads, and thinks *"someone said what I've been feeling."* Every section of every lead magnet must contain at least one underline-line.

The most underline-worthy lines in the VoC data:
- *"Nobody stopped loving each other. Life just got louder."*
- *"We've earned a connection that wasn't existing."*
- *"We talk about the dishwasher."*
- *"All we talk about is the logistics. We never get to the real stuff."*
- *"I feel guilty that he's got his own life and I've got kind of mine."*
