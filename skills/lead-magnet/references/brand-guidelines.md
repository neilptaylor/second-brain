# Brand Guidelines — Me & My Old Man

## Core Brand Essence

**Tagline:** *Stories That Matter. With People You Love.*

**Purpose:** To bring families closer — now — by creating warm, guided spaces where life stories are spoken, heard, and felt together.

**Mission:** Design intimate audio experiences that turn family conversation into connection, understanding, and belonging.

**Vision:** A world where every family makes time to listen — and no voice is lost.

---

## Brand Voice

Warm, grounded, emotionally intelligent. A thoughtful friend who understands what this moment means — not clinical, not corporate, not performative.

**Voice principles:**
- Closeness over cleverness — if a line doesn't deepen connection, delete it
- Plain language, better questions — no jargon, clear concrete prompts
- Invite, include, affirm — shift from "me and you" to "we"
- Present-moment focus — this isn't about preserving the past; it's about connecting now
- Less polish, more presence — conversation, not performance

**Tone in practice:**
- Warm & reassuring: *"This isn't a session to rush — give it the space it deserves."*
- Reflective & grounded: *"What I would give to hear his voice again."*
- Clear & calm: *"No prep. No pressure. Just presence."*
- Emotionally intelligent: *"I regret that I didn't ask, listen, capture more."*

## Updated Positioning (Feb 2026)

Customers do NOT buy 'capture before death.' They buy **'reconnection after natural drift.'**

This is a forward-looking transformation, not a backward-looking preservation.

The audiobook is the BYPRODUCT. The process — the 12 weeks together — IS the product.

> *Nobody stopped loving each other. Life just got louder.*

Families don't fail. They drift. Naturally. Without blame. And they want to find their way back.

## Banned Words — Never Use These

| Banned | Why it fails |
|--------|-------------|
| **Heirloom** | Victorian. Sits in a box. |
| **Keepsake** | Passive. Storing-away energy. |
| **Preserve / Preservation** | Clinical, museum-like. Implies something dying. |
| **Legacy** | Every LinkedIn thought-leader uses this word. Drained of meaning. Use sparingly if at all. |
| **Gift of voice** | Abstract, marketing-y. |
| **Process** | Mechanical. Say *experience* instead. |
| **Treasure / Cherish** | Greeting card language. |
| **For posterity** | No one under 50 uses this. Alienating. |
| **Professional** | Sarah doesn't want polished, she wants human. |
| **Gift for your children** | Too future-focused. Misses the NOW connection need. |
| **Create a legacy for future generations** | Too abstract, too far from daily reality. |
| **Capture / Record / Document (standalone)** | Transactional. Only use paired with connection language: *"capture this moment together."* |
| **Before it's too late (standalone)** | Fear without transformation is just pain. Always pair with possibility. |

## Green Light Language — Use Freely

These come directly from customer transcripts. They convert because they sound like a real person, not a tagline.

| Phrase | Why it works |
|--------|-------------|
| **Naturally drifted** | 'Naturally' removes blame. Families don't fail. They drift. Key repositioning phrase. |
| **Re-center** | *"It just re-centres you as a family."* Active, positive, achievable. |
| **Earned / We earned** | *"We've EARNED a connection."* Not 'got', not 'made'. Implies investment and deserving. |
| **Hook** | *"It gave us a hook to talk about things..."* Practical, non-emotional word for an emotional unlock. |
| **Drift / Drifted** | Most honest word for what happened. No blame. Resonates instantly. |
| **Outside the pod** | Brittany's phrase for when parents feel excluded from adult children's lives. |
| **The real stuff** | *"We never get to the real stuff."* Sarah knows exactly what this means. |
| **Aftermath** | Axel's word for ongoing benefit: *"Connection we're continuing to have aftermath."* |
| **Closed book** | Andy Fahey's phrase — a dad who performs great stories but reveals nothing of himself. |
| **Ripple effect** | Customer language for the halo effect during the 12 weeks. |
| **Why I am like I am** | A parent verbatim — the deepest transformation. Use in testimonials. |
| **The version of him his friends know** | Lisa Nel — the invisible dimension of a parent the family never gets to meet. |
| **Drips and drabs / Little tidbits** | How the Story Hunter describes fragments gathered. Messaging for the non-urgent buyer. |
| **The last one who knows / Holder of that history** | Henrietta's language for specific mortality urgency. More powerful than general urgency. |

## Messaging Pillars

**Pillar 1 — 'Something Real, Before Life Gets Any Louder'**
Gentle urgency rooted in real family drift — and real opportunity to get back to 'the real stuff.' Always pair with the transformation available NOW. Never lead with death or guilt alone.

**Pillar 2 — 'Not Just for the Future. For Right Now.'**
The process IS the product. The 12 weeks of sessions together create the reconnection, not the audiobook at the end.

**Pillar 3 — 'Let Them Be Heard. Truly Heard.'**
Not just recorded — witnessed, received, understood. Parents feel CENTRAL again. Especially powerful for the 'outside the pod' parent.

**Pillar 4 — 'You'll Earn Each Other Back.'**
Source: *"We've earned a connection that wasn't existing."* — Axel. Reframes the price as relationship ROI, not product ROI.

**Pillar 5 — 'We Make It Easy. You Make It Meaningful.'**
No prep. No pressure. Just presence. Facilitation as the bridge, not the destination.

**Pillar 6 — 'Your Siblings Will Thank You.'**
Sibling reconnection is the transformation customers MOST thank you for — but don't expect going in. The sibling who resists most often becomes the sibling who benefits most. Make this a primary benefit, not a bonus.

## Brand QC Checklist

Before publishing anything, ask:
1. **Closeness test:** Does this increase love, belonging, or connection?
2. **Plain English:** Jargon removed?
3. **Banned terms:** None of the banned list present?
4. **Action bias:** Does the CTA invite listening together before purchase?
5. **Tone audit:** Warm, reflective, with positive/active energy?
6. **'We' not 'you':** Does it create shared experience?
7. **Brittany test:** Raw, honest about family complexity? Sounds like a real person?
8. **Axel test:** About the surprising sibling benefit, not just expected parent stories? Uses 'earned' not 'got'?
9. **Jane test:** Makes parents feel CENTRAL, not just documented?
