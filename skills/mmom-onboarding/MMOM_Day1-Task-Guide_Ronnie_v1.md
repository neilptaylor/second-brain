## FINAL HOUR — Make your first clip

**Goal:** Produce one test audiogram. Not for publishing — just for Neil to review on the call.

**This is about getting your first feel for the workflow, not perfection.**

**Step 1: Find a great 60–90 second story**
Use the Riverside **Co-Creator** and/or browse the **Made for You** suggestions for your chosen family. You're looking for:
- A moment that's warm, funny, or emotionally resonant
- Clean audio (no major crosstalk or background noise)
- A complete thought — it has a beginning and end

Good prompts for Co-Creator: *"a funny childhood story"*, *"a surprising memory"*, *"a touching moment about family"*

**Step 2: Export the clip from Riverside**
Once you've identified your moment:
- Trim to the exact start and end (leave a breath at each end — don't cut too tight)
- Export as audio

**Step 3: Create a test audiogram in Canva**
Using the MMOM brand colours and font:
- Black background
- Caption text on screen (transcribe the quote or use Riverside's auto-transcript)
- Keep it simple for now — we'll refine the template together

Don't add intro/outro music yet — that comes once we've confirmed the format is right.

**Step 4: Save your files**
Save everything in the Google Drive folder Neil set up for you. Name the file:

## Quick reference — Content types cheat sheet

| Type | Length | Format | Complexity |
|------|--------|--------|------------|
| **Audiogram** | 60–120s | Black screen + caption + music | Low |
| **Video Clip / Reel** | 60–90s | Session footage + cards | Low–Medium |
| **Audio Highlight** | 60–120s | Audio only + music | Low |
| **Case Study Clip** | 90–120s | 3 clips stitched together | Medium |

**Start with:** Audiograms. They're the simplest format and let you learn Riverside and Canva without worrying about video editing.

**Build to:** Case Study Clips. These take more time but are the most powerful content.
