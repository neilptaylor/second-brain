## First — a bit about why this exists

In 2021, my dad passed away.

Six days later, my son was born.

In the same week, I lost the person whose voice I most wanted my son to hear — and I realised I'd never properly captured it. The stories, the specific details, the way he'd tell a joke, the hesitation before a punchline. Gone.

I'd been meaning to. I just never made the time.

Me & My Old Man grew out of that grief. And out of a belief that most of us are doing the same thing — drifting past the people who matter most, telling ourselves we'll have the conversation later.

We're here to make sure families don't have to feel what I felt.

---

## What we actually do

Over 12 weeks, we sit with families and capture their parents' life stories.

Here's how it works:

**Week 1–2:** I interview the adult children (1 hour each) — their hopes, what they want to learn, what they're a little afraid to ask.

**Weeks 3–10:** Three two-hour sessions with the parent. Their whole life, in their own voice, at their own pace. Childhood. School. First love. Work. The stories they've never been asked to tell.

**Final session:** A Family Wrap Up — children and parent together, sharing what they felt, what surprised them, what changed.

Then our editors shape all of it into a finished audio documentary. Chapter by chapter. With music. With the natural rhythm of real conversation — stutters, laughter, pauses and all. Because that's the voice. That's what people want to hear again, one day.

We've now done this with 32 families across the UK, Australia, Canada, and beyond. 350 hours of interviews. Hundreds of stories that would otherwise have been lost.

## The content types you'll be working with

You'll be creating four types of content from the Riverside recordings. Study these carefully — they're the heart of Task Area 1.

**Audiograms**
Black screen. Caption only. Intro/outro music. The story does all the work. These are for emotive moments — a son describing what it meant to hear his mum's story, a parent recalling their first day of school. Simple visually. Powerful emotionally. Great for families who are camera-shy about consent.

**Video Clips / Reels**
Actual session footage, cut and cleaned. Start card, end card with the website. Brand colours, brand font. These become social content and referral gifts.

**Audio Highlights**
A standalone audio story. The clip, intro music in, outro music out. No visuals.

**Case Study Clips**
This is the more involved one — and the most powerful. Three parts stitched together:
- Child's opening (30s from the Child Briefing session) — their hopes and motivations going in
- Parent's story (60s from one of the three Parent sessions) — something that connects to that motivation
- Child's reflection (from the Wrap Up session) — what it meant

Here's a reference example: [meandmyoldman.co.uk/abel](http://www.meandmyoldman.co.uk/abel) — watch it before you start. It shows the before/after journey in under 2 minutes.

## The brand — what it looks and feels like

**Tone:** Warm, intimate, private. Never showy. Every piece of content should feel like something you'd want to pass to a family member, not broadcast to a crowd.

**Our tagline:** *Stories that matter. With people you love.*

**What we never say:** preserve, legacy, heirloom, keepsake, treasure. These words look backwards. Our work is about right now — the relationship that deepens this week, the laugh you didn't expect, the story you didn't know existed.

**The voice:** Natural. Human. The recordings aren't over-edited. Stutters stay in. Long pauses stay in. Because that's the person. That's what makes it precious.
