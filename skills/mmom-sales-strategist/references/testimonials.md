# Testimonials Library & Deployment Strategy

## Deployment Rules

- **One testimonial per touchpoint maximum** — never stack them
- **In proposals: never use the same person's testimonial more than once across the whole deck** — if Chris Walton appears on slide 6, he cannot appear on slide 8. Pick a different person for each placement.
- Match the testimonial to the prospect's specific situation
- Use as social proof, not as padding
- Let the testimonial do the work — don't over-explain it

## Referral Strategy

**Immediate post-project sequence:**
1. Referral ask first (before testimonial request)
2. Low-friction ask: "Forward this video memento to one person you think would love to hear it"
3. If they engage, follow up with testimonial Zoom request (15 mins)

**Most effective referral framing:**
- "Is there one person in your life who you think would love to hear this?" (not "do you know anyone?")
- Give them the exact words to use when forwarding
- Father's Day, Mother's Day, Christmas: natural calendar triggers for referral re-engagement

**Dormant referral re-engagement:**
- Switch to a different channel
- Lead with the content (a memento, a clip, a post) not a request
- Calendar trigger as the excuse to reach out
- Remove the ask; reconnect first
