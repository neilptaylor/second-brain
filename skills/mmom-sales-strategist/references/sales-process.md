# Sales Process Reference

## Call Structure: The Family Story Discovery Session (60 mins)

### Pre-Call Mindset
- You're not selling, you're serving
- Listen for the WHY — the trigger event, the regret they're avoiding
- Hold the space — they may get emotional; that's the signal
- Trust the transformation — they want permission to care

### Part 1: Discovery (20 mins)

**Opening:** Set the frame — "I want to understand what's brought you here, hear about your parent, understand what you're hoping happens, and if I can help, show you exactly how."

**Key questions:**
- "What brings you here today?"
- "Was there a specific moment when you thought — I need to do this now?" (The trigger event — dig into this)
- "Tell me about your family — siblings? Which parent?"
- "What are they like?"

**The Dream State — two versions:**
- Near-term: "It's Christmas this year. You're sitting together and instead of surface chat, your parent is opening up about their childhood… What would that mean to you?"
- Long-term: "It's Christmas 15–20 years from now. Your kids are older. Your parent isn't here anymore, but their voice is…"

**The Emotional Anchor:**
"If you don't do this — and time just keeps passing — what does that cost you?"
[Let the weight sit. This is where the sale happens.]

**Support structure:**
- "Have you talked to your mum/dad about this yet?"
- "What about your siblings?"
- "And your spouse/partner — where are they on this?"

### Part 2: The Bridge & Pitch (25 mins)

**Credibility Bridge (3 mins):** Neil's story — losing his dad in 2021, three years of dementia, the failed attempt to capture his story, his father's voice fading.

**The Mechanism — Three Phases:**

**Phase 1: Setup**
- 1-hour Project Planning Call (all siblings + parent minimum)
- Shared folder, Reflection Primer, homework assignments 48hrs before interviews
- "By the end of this call, the hard part is done. Everything is scheduled."

**Phase 2: Production**
- Children's interviews (1hr each): What do you hope to hear about your parent's life?
- Parent sessions (3 × 2hrs = 6hrs): "In the first hour we blow off the cobwebs. By hour five or six we find the dusty box in the corner — that's where the gold is."
- Independent listening: 6–8 hour audiobook, 8 chapters, 45 mins each
- Reflection session (1–1.5hrs): Guided family debrief

**Phase 3: Delivery**
- Full audiobook as private playlist with custom artwork
- 5 audio highlights (1–2 mins)
- 3 video mementos (1–2 mins, photos + voice)

**The Investment:** "Everything I just described is £5,000. I know that's not a small number. But I lost my dad, and I would pay double — triple — to have what I'm offering you right now."

[Let it breathe. Don't justify. Don't apologize.]

"How does that land for you?"

### Part 3: Objection Handling & Close (15 mins)

**Temperature check:** "How are you feeling about all of this?"

**Isolation technique:** "X aside — do you feel this is everything you need to get to [goal]?"

**The Close (if ready):**
"So it sounds like this is something you want to do. Is that right?"
→ Two options: Full payment £5,000 (save £152) or three monthly payments £1,717.33/month (£5,152 total)

**If they need time:**
"Take 24–48 hours. But here's what I want you to do: picture yourself in five years. Your parent is gone. Ask yourself — will I regret not doing this?"
[7-day price hold: after that, investment goes to £5,500]

## Post-Call Follow-Up Process

### Immediate (Within 2 Hours)
Email template structure:
- Subject: "Our conversation today — [NAME]'s story"
- Opening: reflect the specific emotional beat they shared
- What we covered: their situation in their words
- What we'd create: bullet version of the experience
- Two payment options with links
- 7-day pricing deadline
- Warm close

### Follow-Up Sequence (No Response)
- Day 3: "Still thinking it through?"
- Day 5: "Quick reminder — 2 days left at this price"
- Day 7: "A quick story about a family just like yours"
- Day 14: "The cost of waiting"

### Channel Switching (After Multiple No-Responses)
If same-channel follow-ups have gone unanswered:
1. Switch channel (email → WhatsApp, or vice versa)
2. Remove the ask entirely
3. Re-engage around a calendar trigger or piece of content
4. Reconnect as a human before reconnecting as a founder

## Pricing Logic

### Single Parent
- Full experience: £5,000 (standard)
- Payment plan: 3 × £1,717.33 = £5,152 total

### Two Parents / Full Family
- Full experience: £7,500–£8,500
- Payment plan: adds ~6% to total

### Anchoring
- "I would pay double — triple — to have what I'm offering you right now."
- Compare to family holiday: similar cost, connection happens "almost by accident" vs. MMOM guarantees depth as its entire purpose.
- After-the-fact: "I'd have paid ten times more" (Dominic McMullan)

### Price Objection Framework
1. Ask what specifically feels like a lot
2. Isolate whether it's genuine budget, timing, family buy-in, or spouse
3. Never reduce the experience — protect the fullness
4. Offer payment plan as a convenience, not a discount
5. Bring in the cost of NOT doing this

## Proposal Rules

- **Single bespoke proposal** — never tiered options
- Open with their situation, mirroring their own words back
- Show you were listening: reference specific details from the discovery call
- Price presented confidently, without apology or pre-emptive justification
- One testimonial included — matched to their specific situation
- Clear next step (one action, not multiple)

---

## Red Flags on a Sales Call

- Vague answers ("I don't know, just seems nice")
- No emotional connection to parent ("They're fine, nothing special")
- Price shopping ("What's your cheapest option?")
- Timeline too far out ("Maybe next year")
- Key decision-maker not on the call and not yet informed

## Green Flags on a Sales Call

- They got emotional (tears = connection to the transformation)
- They talked more than you did
- They brought up fears without prompting (trust established)
- They asked "When can we start?"
- They named a specific trigger event with emotion attached
