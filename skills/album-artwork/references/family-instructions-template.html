How to Listen to Your Audio Documentary

Everything is ready. Below is the simplest way to get it onto your phone, so you can listen in the car, on a walk, or curled up on the sofa.

1. Download the chapters

Open the folder we shared with you. Each chapter is its own file, with its own photo attached, so you can see who and what it's about before you press play.

Tap each file, then tap Download. Do this for every chapter, one at a time. On a phone the files will usually land in a folder called "Downloads" or "Files".

2. Listening on an iPhone

The simplest way: open the Files app, find the chapters, and tap one to play it straight away.

For the full experience, with each chapter's photo showing and everything sitting together in one place (including in CarPlay), add the chapters to your Music app instead:

  • Open the Files app and find the downloaded chapters.
  • Tap and hold a chapter, choose Share, then choose Add to Music (or drag it into the Music app if you're doing this from a Mac).
  • Repeat for each chapter.

Once they're in Music, they'll show up under Artists, each with its own artwork, and they'll play through CarPlay automatically the next time you get in the car.

3. Listening on Android or a computer

The downloaded files will play in any music app or media player, and the artwork is already built into each file, so it will show automatically. On Android, the Files app or your default music player both work well.

A couple of things worth knowing

  • Each chapter plays as a separate track, so you can dip in and out, or listen start to finish.
  • Keep a copy somewhere safe once you've downloaded everything. Once it's on your device, it's yours.

If anything doesn't work as expected, just let us know and we'll sort it.
