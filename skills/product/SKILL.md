# Product

Owns how an MMOM product works from the client's side. Selling it belongs to the sales skills. Writing about it belongs to the content skills. This skill decides what happens, in what order, and what the client fills in, hears and receives.

## Before you start

1. `knowledge/offer/pricing.md` for the product's current terms (length, deliverable, turnaround, price). Never restate a term from memory.
2. `knowledge/voice/VOICE_PROFILE_Neil_Taylor.md` in full, for any words a client will read.
3. `knowledge/lessons.md`.

## Principles

- **Design backwards from the deliverable.** Every form question and every call segment must feed something that ends up in the finished audio. If it doesn't, cut it.
- **One flow, three artefacts.** The form, the call and the prep sheet share one structure, so Neil can read a form response straight down the page as his running order.
- **Forms are short and fun.** A question should make someone smile or stop and think. Mix quick ticks with a few open questions. Ask for one concrete thing (a photo, a phrase, a moment) rather than "tell me about".
- **Facts the form gathers are facts Neil doesn't spend call time on.** Names, ages, dates go in the form. The call is for stories.
- **Tone is uplifting and reflective.** Pain is the doorway, never the destination. No corporate words (goals, objectives, outcomes, journey).
- **Say it's private.** Any client form states that what they write and record is private to them, and theirs to use as they wish. Any wider use (e.g. Neil's book) is an optional, anonymous opt-in at the end.
- **If Neil is cut from the edit, design for self-contained answers.** Prompts not questions, name the thing in the prompt, brief the client before recording to fold the question into their answer.
- **No vague timings to clients.** "Very soon", never "in a few weeks" when the product promises a quick turnaround.
- **Every prep sheet carries one interviewing skill to practise**, named, with the exact words Neil would use. One, not five.
- **Prep sheets and briefing docs are 2 pages maximum.** Neil reads them on the call. Condense the form answers to the line he needs, keep the verbatim responses in a separate source doc. Export the Google Doc to PDF and count pages before reporting done. Heading 1 is 16pt and Heading 2 is 14pt.
