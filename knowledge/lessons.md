## Recurring patterns to watch (seeded from Voice Profile review)

These aren't logged mistakes yet — they're pre-emptive rules based on known tendencies:

- **AI-sounding phrases**: "stopped me cold", "gold" (exclamation), "sit with", "how it landed" — never use these
- **American marketing cadence**: If it sounds like an email drip from a US info-product creator, it's wrong for Neil
- **Vague emotion over specific detail**: If there's no dishwasher, no 12-minute phone call, no fluffy tufts of hair — it's not specific enough yet
- **Fear without hope**: Regret language must always be paired with possibility. Grief is the doorway, not the destination
- **Pointing fingers**: Never "you're too busy" — always "we". Neil is in the sandwich generation too
- **Selling before earning trust**: For a £4,800 emotional experience, trust comes first. Always.
