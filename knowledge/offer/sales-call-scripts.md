Neil's own description: these are the basis of sales calls, but not yet a workflow or a skill. They are reference material, not an automation.

## Why this file exists

The systems map records the Sale stage as well covered because four skills touch it. That is only true for what happens around the call: the proposal, the follow-up, the voice check. The call itself runs off these scripts and off Neil, and nothing in the repo points at them.

## What to do with them next

Read them before building anything that touches the Sale stage, especially:

- Any Enquiry system, because whatever it sends has to hand over cleanly into the opening of a sales call.
- The between-calls VSL, since the price reveal it carries has to match how price is introduced in the script.
- Any future sales-call skill. Extract the script into the repo first, then build on it. Do not write a new one from scratch.
