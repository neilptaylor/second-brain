## Price band rules

- TARGET is 5% off LIST. That is where most deals are designed to land, not conceded to.
- FLOOR is 7.5% off LIST. The walk-away line. Never below.
- Minimum acceptable margin on a full-cost basis is 50%.
- A second parent is priced at 95% of the first.

Margins at LIST run 60% to 66% across the board, so there is room to move inside the band.

## Chapters

One chapter per recorded strand, plus one for the wrap-up.

- Parent's own 3-session set: 6, times parents recorded
- Buyer's own hour, option B only: 1, times parents recorded
- Children's group sitting, option C only: 1 per family, does not double for two parents
- Each child's own session, option D only: 1 per child per parent
- Family wrap-up: 1 per family, options B C and D, never A

Totals, one parent: A 6, B 8, C 8, D 9 to 11. Two parents: A 12, B 15, C 14, D 17 to 21.
