# Snapshot, batch 2: people who have lost both parents. Draft v1

Parked until the founding five are done. Send personally, one at a time, to people Neil knows have lost both parents. Never as a broadcast, never tagged into an automation. Start with subscribers Neil knows personally.

Why this segment: Snapshot is the only thing MMOM sells that they can buy. Alex Cheyney named it at her handover (17 July 2026): "I've got quite a few friends whose parents are already dead." Until now, that was a dead end for referrals.

The angle: they are the oldest generation in their family now. They're the one who'll be asked about, not the one asking.

Rules: no urgency, no "before it's too late". Neil's own loss comes first, so it never reads as though he's using theirs. Use catch, keep or share, never "tell", for what happens in a Snapshot.

---

## Personal email / message

**Subject:** Something I've been meaning to send you

Hi [name],

You know what I do with families: I record parents in their seventies and eighties, so their children have them in their own words.

You'll also know better than most why I started. My dad died in 2021, and there's so much I never asked him. His voice is going too, which I didn't expect.

Something's been on my mind, and I thought of you.

When both your parents have gone, you become the one the family will one day wonder about. The questions we never got to ask ours, somebody will want to ask us. Where we grew up, what we were scared of, what our mum and dad were actually like. You're the last person who can answer some of those.

So I've built something called Snapshot. An hour and a half with me, online, about your life. Your parents can come into it as much as you'd like, or not at all. You get about an hour of edited audio back within 7 days, for you, and for whoever comes after.

It's £300. If it's something you'd want, reply and we'll find a time. If not, I'm just glad to have been in touch.

Neil

P.S. [Personal line about their mum or dad, only if Neil knew them. Otherwise leave it out.]
