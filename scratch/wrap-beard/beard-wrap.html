Getting it onto your phone

Downloading

1

Download

Open the Stories folder. Tap each chapter, then Download. On a computer, click the arrow next to the folder name and choose Download to get all nine at once.

2

iPhone

Quickest: open the Files app and tap a chapter to play. For the full experience, with the artwork and CarPlay, tap and hold each chapter, choose Share, then Add to Music. On a Mac, ctrl + click the files and open with Music.

3

Android or PC

The files play in any music app or media player. The artwork is built into each file, so it shows up on its own.

Added them on your Mac?

Getting them onto your iPhone

On your Mac

  1. Open Music.
  2. Go to Music → Settings → General.
  3. Tick Sync Library.
  4. Check you're signed into the same Apple Account as your iPhone.
  5. Add the chapters to Music, if you haven't already.
  6. Give it a few minutes to sync to your iCloud Music Library.

On your iPhone

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Music.
  3. Turn Sync Library on.
  4. Open the Music app.
  5. Go to Library → Albums and look for Richard's.

No cables needed. You don't have to plug the phone into the Mac.

Listening and sharing

Pass it round the family

Sharing the folder

  1. Keep everything in Google Drive.
  2. Click the three dots next to the folder.
  3. Click Share → Copy link.
  4. Send it to the family.
  5. They download, they listen. Done.

A note on Spotify

I'd steer clear of Spotify for this. It needs setting up on every device separately, and it won't share between family members unless you pay for a hosting platform. The Google Drive route is much smoother for everyone.

Keeping it safe

Save it everywhere.

I'd love each of you to keep your own copy of the chapters and mementos, in as many places as you can: your computer, Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud, a USB stick, an external hard drive.

And put it in your wills. I mean it.
