# The Front Door At Eight (Pain Points, Thu 26 Nov)

Source: Aneta Ardelian Kuzma podcast doc, newsletter 3 (the five-senses question, the roses, the bath door). Not in Notion or Kit as of 7 Oct 2026.

```
I once spent a whole twelve-minute call with my mum on the dishwasher.

Nothing was wrong.
It's just where the conversation goes.
How's the knee.
Did you see the match.
Is the boiler sorted.

We stay in the lanes we've always driven in, because we think we already know the answers.

Here's the one question I ask every parent before anything else:

"Think of the house you lived in when you were eight. Stand outside the front door. Now walk me through it, using all five senses."

One woman got as far as the blue front door and the roses beside it, and stopped.

"Actually, I think that might be my first memory. The smell of roses."

Her children were in the next room. They had no idea.

Another took me into her family's kitchen in the 1950s. There was a bath in it.
They put a door over the bath and ate on it.
Then the door came off, and they bathed in the kitchen, because it was the warmest room in the house.

I was born in the eighties. I'd never have thought to ask.

You don't need the right question for a whole life.
You need one that gets them to the front door.

Save this for your next call with them.
```
