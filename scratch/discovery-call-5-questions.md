# Discovery call: 5 questions

Homework for the family before the call — five questions for them to think about, sent as-is.

1. **What's changed?** What's brought you to this now, and is this the first time you've looked into something like this — or have you tried anything already, writing it down yourselves or looking at another company?

2. **Anything we should know?** Are there any health concerns, or any particular urgency, that would be worth us knowing about before we talk?

3. **Have you spoken to your parent about it yet?** How did that land?

4. **Who else needs to be part of this decision, and what do you think their first question will be?**

5. **Picture it finished.** You're all listening to it in a year, and your parent isn't here any more — what do you want your kids to have?

---

## Alternative opening hooks

1. "Five questions, ten minutes, and you'll walk into the call already knowing what to say."
2. "The more honest you are here, the less we need to cover live, this is what shapes the whole project."
