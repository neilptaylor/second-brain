# PR pitch: Stories of those we've lost (rewrite after Katy Evans call, 9 Oct)

**Subject:** "What was Dad like?" An audio documentary for two children who never met him

Hi [name],

A woman took her case to the Court of Session in Edinburgh to have children with her late husband. She won, and it made the [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2020/sep/15/scotland-woman-seeks-legal-approval-to-use-late-husbands-sperm-ivf) and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-scotland-north-east-orkney-shetland-54294414). Last year, with those two children still young, she asked me to capture who he was.

I interviewed his parents, his two brothers, three of his friends and her, and built an audio documentary with his laugh in it, and his silly guitar songs. So when the children ask "what was Dad like", there is an answer.

Grief Awareness Week starts on 2 December. I can put you in touch with her, or give you the documentary to listen to and write about, whichever suits you or one of your writers. [Here's how she felt about it.](https://www.meandmyoldman.co.uk/#stories-of-those-weve-lost)

I'm Neil, I run Me & My Old Man, and I started it after my dad died in March 2021 and my son was born six days later. If it's a fit, I'd love to talk.

Neil

## Before it goes

- Confirm she's happy to be contacted by a journalist. The call only had her saying she'd come on the couch for the One Show, and she doesn't want the wider family pushed to listen yet.
- Katy's two options (talk to her, or write about the documentary) are both in the third paragraph. The court case is the recognition hook, not the story. The story is him.
- Same shape works for the Family Documentary Christmas gift-list pitch: swap the case for a family, keep the two options. Say if you want that version.
