## The one rule that cuts the list

Money Models: attraction, then upsell, then continuity, in that order, and "perfect one offer until it is reliable before the next." You have six founders who haven't had their Snapshot yet. So nothing you build tomorrow is final. Everything below is a test, not a launch.

## 1. One product, two ways to buy it

| | Price | Margin on £145 cost |
|---|---|---|
| Single Snapshot | £399 | 63.7% |
| Snapshot every year (billed yearly) | £299 | 51.5% |

Why: Hormozi prices the one-off 1.33x to 2.66x the continuity price so most people choose continuity. £399 vs £299 is 1.33x. The single price exists mainly to make the annual one look obvious.

Elasticity test: this is it. If people baulk at £299 a year, you learn it from real buyers, not guesses. Don't go under £290 a year, that's your 50% floor.

Note: pricing.md says £300 single. This replaces it if you agree. Update pricing.md, don't leave two numbers.

## 2. The founding six

Drop the "refer three friends to keep £99" idea. Too many moving parts, and it makes their price depend on a chore.

Instead: **Founders' rate, £249 a year, locked for five years. Plus a free year for every friend who books.**

- £249 is 42% margin, under your 50% rule. Deliberate exception, six people, worth it for the testimonials and the referrals.
- Ask after their feedback call, when they've just heard it. Not before.
- Partner use: yes, "this year's your turn" is a lovely line. One Snapshot per year per account.

## 3. Kevin, and the Steve influencer play

Give his readers a bonus, not a discount. Hormozi's thinking: discounts cheapen, bonuses add.

- Readers pay £299 a year or £399 single, same as everyone. Their bonus: the video version included.
- Kevin gets £50 per booking. You keep £104 on an annual (36%) after him. Fine, because he's acquisition you'd otherwise pay for.
- Kevin himself gets a Snapshot free. Not a five-year locked deal. Simpler to say yes to.

**Tomorrow, first call: don't take the structure.** Do Steve's move. Tell him what it is, ask what he'd pay, then offer him his own one free. The affiliate conversation happens after he's heard his. A man who's just cried at his own Snapshot sells it better than any blurb.

## 4. Will it undercut the documentary?

Only if they look like the same thing. They're not.

- Snapshot is about you, this year. Light edit, about an hour.
- The documentary is about your parent, their whole life. Full production, a team.

And make Snapshot point upwards: a **rollover credit**. Anyone who's bought a Snapshot gets £300 off a family documentary. Hormozi's rule is the next offer must be at least 4x the credit; £2,400 clears it. That turns the price gap into a ladder, not a comparison.

## 5. Geoff's drop-in

Park it. It's not a new product, it's a reason to book the same one. Use it as a line in the copy ("a year, or a moment") and see if anyone bites. Sample size of one doesn't get its own price.
