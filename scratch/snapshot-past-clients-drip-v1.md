## Email 3 — Day 8

**Subject:** Four hours in, and he'd never told anyone
**Preview:** Not even his own son.

Hey,

A story I think about often.

Early on, I sat with a dad for four hours across two sessions. He was a natural storyteller: warm, articulate, generous with everything. Childhood, career, marriage, fatherhood. I thought we were done.

Then in the final session he started talking about a dream he'd had about his own dad. In it, his dad was wearing the green tweed jacket he always wore. He held out his hand. They just started walking.

He'd never told anyone that dream. Not his wife. Not his son.

It took four hours of space for it to surface. Not because he was hiding it. Because nobody had ever made the room for it to come out.

That's what a sitting like this actually does. It isn't really about the questions. It's about the space.

You gave your own parents that room already. Snapshot is the same room, just for you, thirty years earlier than most people ever get it.

If you want to hear what it's like from the other side, hit reply and I'll set you up.

Neil

P.S. He told his son afterward. His son had never heard it either. That's the bit I keep coming back to: the story was always there. It just needed somewhere to go.
