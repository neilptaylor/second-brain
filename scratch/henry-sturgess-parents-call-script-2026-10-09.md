# Henry Sturgess and his parents: 30-minute call

**Headline: By the end, they have everything they need to say yes or no, and Henry knows what he is taking back to them.**

Format: Hero Story only. Henry circles back with his folks over the weekend and agrees whether to go ahead. Ask Henry before you start whether he wants questions first or the walk-through first.

## 1. Open (3 min)
- Thank them for the time. Tell Mum and Dad this is theirs: stop me whenever.
- "Henry's told me a bit about you both. Dad, the choir and starting work at 16. Mum, growing up across Africa."
- Check what they've seen so far. Mum has read the site but not the question list.

## 2. Why Henry wants this (3 min)
Let Henry say it in his own words, then echo it back.
- "I see friends whose parents pass away, and the story only gets captured almost at the funeral."
- "Hey Dad and Mum, I want to give a present here. A family present."
- He lives in Brazil and is home every quarter. The stories that fall out over a pub lunch are the ones he misses.

## 3. Bust the two worries early (4 min)
- "It's not about telling funny stories, and nobody needs to be funny."
- Dad: this is a project for the winter, and he does not have to carry it alone. Mum can help him prepare, and a good session is one where I bring him back if he loses his thread.
- Sensitivities: "Is there anything you'd rather I stayed clear of?" Dad has said he's open about the 1980s. Say it is a chapter, not the focus, and this is not therapy.

## 4. Walk through Hero Story (10 min)
1. Child briefing from Henry, so I arrive prepared and nobody is put on the spot.
2. Reflection guide, the audio version, and a life chart for each of you.
3. Three 2-hour sessions each with me, 12 chapters in all, edited and scored.
4. Henry's own hour with each of you, three chapters. This is his gift: you hear what he wants to ask.
5. A 1.5-hour wrap-up together once you've listened.
- Audio only, private, downloaded to Henry's device. Nothing goes on Spotify or YouTube.
- Twelve weeks start to finish. Neil is the one person they deal with throughout.

## 5. The guarantee (1 min)
Full refund if it doesn't feel right after the kickoff call and before the first recorded session. 35 families, never invoked.

## 6. Their questions (5 min)
Ask: "What would you want to know that I haven't covered?" Then stop talking.

## 7. Close (4 min)
- Ask Henry: "Do you want to talk through the investment with everyone, or just us afterwards?"
- "You've now got what you need to decide. What day will you let me know?" Get the day said out loud.
- Only then, if it helps him say yes: the grandchildren questions for his daughter, and audio clips from their stories, both included if he books by that day. Say why: my October slots and Dad's winter timing.

## Don't
- No pitch on price in front of the parents unless Henry asks.
- No target price, no instalments, unless he raises the money.
- Never push Dad's memory. If it comes up, Mum helps with prep and I keep the pace.
