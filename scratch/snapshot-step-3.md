## 1. Positioning, one paragraph

The stories you'll wish you remembered in thirty years are happening this week. The school gate on the first day. The best afternoon of the summer. The thing your seven-year-old said on Tuesday that you'll have lost by Christmas. Snapshot is an hour and a half with me, about your life as it is right now. I help you prepare, I ask the questions nobody around you has time to ask, and within a week you get it back as about an hour of edited audio and a handful of short video clips. It isn't coaching. It isn't therapy. It's just space, and your stories.

## 2. Pricing: needs your call

V10.12 has Snapshot at **£300**, one session, full cost £145, margin 51.7%. It has no two-a-year or four-a-year price. The plan's £250 / ~£400 / ~£500 predates V10 and fails the 50% floor at every point:

| Plan figure | Per session | Cost | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|
| £250 one-off | £250 | £145 | 42.0% |
| ~£400 for 2 a year | £200 | £290 | 27.5% |
| ~£500 for 4 a year | £125 | £580 | -16.0% |

The cost does not fall with tenure. Every session is 1.5 hours of your time at £80 plus an hour of editing at £25. So any per-session discount under £290 breaks the floor.

Three ways through:

1. **One price only for now.** £300 a session. Decide on bundles after the pilot, once you know whether people come back. Also fits "not a subscription".
2. **Bundles at the floor.** £300 · £580 for 2 · £1,160 for 4. Exactly 50% on the bundles, no headroom.
3. **Make repeat sessions cheaper to deliver.** Returning sessions at 1 hour instead of 1.5 (cost £105, floor £210). Then something like £300 · £550 for 2 · £1,000 for 4 clears 50%.

## 3. Deliverable and true cost per session

- 1.5 hours recorded in Riverside, online.
- Out: about an hour of long-edit audio, plus a selection of video clips, leaning on Riverside's automated "made for you" clips.
- Neil edits, about 1 hour. No editor.
- Back to the client within one week.

True cost per session, from V10.12 Bolt-Ons row 8: **£145** (1.5 session hrs at £80 = £120, plus 1 ops hr at £25 = £25, editor £0). Cash cost is nil beyond the Riverside subscription, which is not yet allocated in the model (Controls, "Other software / platforms", still empty).
