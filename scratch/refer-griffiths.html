What it is

Your mum or dad's stories. In their voice. To keep.

There's a version of your own mum or dad you've never fully met. This is how you meet them, while they're still here to tell it. Not the headline facts. The texture underneath. Delivering papers to Althorp with his own dad. The proposal that never quite happened. A 50p Polo frisbee that sent his wife, then him, out into the sea.

Six to sixteen hours of recorded conversation, led personally by Neil Taylor. Shaped into chapters. Delivered as an audio documentary the whole family keeps, in their own voices, over the old photographs, and passes on. Most projects run 4 to 16 weeks start to finish.
