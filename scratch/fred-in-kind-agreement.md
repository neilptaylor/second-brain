# Neil & Fred: what we could make for each other

*Working draft for our session, 6 November 2026*

## First things first: friendship comes first

None of this is worth it if it gets in the way of us. That's the whole principle.

So we keep it simple. We're open about what's possible, what each of us can actually do, and when. If something gets too much, we say so early, over a pint, not in a stiff email.

And we accept now that it won't be perfectly even. One of us will probably put in more hours than the other at some point. A day of shooting and fifteen hours of interviews aren't the same currency, and we're not going to try to make them one. That's okay. We agree to be generous rather than precise.

**Three ground rules**

- Say it straight: what we can do, what we can't, and by when.
- No scorekeeping. If it feels lopsided, we talk about it, we don't stew on it.
- Either of us can pause or step back, no hard feelings, friendship intact.
