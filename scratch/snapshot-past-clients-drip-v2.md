# Snapshot drip for Past Clients, v2 (emails 1, 2 and 5)

Built on Neil's reviewed v1 (Drive, 28 Sep). Three emails, Day 0 / 4 / 11. Send to the children who bought, not the parents we recorded. Leave out Griffiths and Beard, who hear about it personally.

What changed from v1: audio leads, and video is a £50 add-on. The "handful of short clips" is gone. The founding five is added to email 2. There's a future scene in email 1. The P.S. on email 5 is swapped out, because "no rush" is flag-and-deny.

---

## Email 1, Day 0

**Subject:** The thing I built after your family's story
**Preview:** Not about your parents this time.

Hey,

I've spent 390 plus hours now sitting across from people in their seventies and eighties, listening to them tell the story of their life. Yours included.

Somewhere in all those hours, a thought turned up that I couldn't quite put down again.

By the time I'm sitting with someone at seventy five, the school run is fifty years behind them.
The best summer of their life has gone soft round the edges.
They remember that it happened.
They've lost what it felt like.

My dad died in 2021. I know roughly what he did in his forties.
I have no idea what it felt like to be him then.
And the sound of his voice is going too. That's the bit I mind most.

So I built something for the middle of a life, not just the end of one.
It's called Snapshot.

An hour and a half with me, online, about your life exactly as it is now.
Not your parents' story this time. Yours.

Picture one of your kids, thirty-odd years from now, about the age you are today. Kettle on, earphones in. They press play, and there you are. A bit knackered, laughing about the morning they refused to wear shoes to school. Saying what kept you up at night, and what you hoped for them, long before they'd have understood a word of it.

That's what you'd be giving them. And you'd get to hear it first.

I'll be back in a few days with what it actually involves.

Neil

P.S. I'm starting with five, all from families I've already worked with. If you already know you want in, hit reply and I'll get you booked before I've finished explaining it properly.

## Email 2, Day 4

**Subject:** What Snapshot actually is
**Preview:** Half the length of what your family already did.

Hey,

Here's the actual shape of it.

Snapshot is one sitting.
An hour and a half, online, wherever you can get quiet: the kitchen table after the kids are down, the car in the school car park, wherever.

I ask, you talk.
Before we start, I'll send you a bit to think about, so you're not starting cold. You know the score.
Then I listen properly, the way I did with your mum and dad, and nobody interrupts.

Within a week you get it back: about an hour of edited audio. Your voice, your stories, ready for the car or the kitchen.

Want the video too, so the kids can see your face and not just hear it? That's £50 more.

It isn't coaching.
It isn't therapy.
It's just your stories.
There's no homework after.

One sitting, £300, back to you within the week.

**The founding five**

The first five people through are my founding group. You pay the same £300. Afterwards I'll ask you for two things: a short video on what it was like, and fifteen minutes of honest feedback on what worked and what didn't. In return, the video version is on me.

If any of that is what you've been meaning to do for yourself, hit reply and I'll find you a date.

Neil

P.S. The questions are different to the ones I ask your parents. These are about being in the middle of it, not looking back on it.
