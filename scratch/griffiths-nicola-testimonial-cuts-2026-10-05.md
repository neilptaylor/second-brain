## 2. Riverside clips (one speaker, continuous)

**A. The hour (hero, /refer-griffiths)** · in 03:28, out 04:05 (~35s)
> "The process was so lovely because being able to sit down and talk with you about dad for like an hour, I don't think I'd envisaged how lovely that would be. I knew that it would be fun and it would be interesting… But I don't think I knew how much it would mean to just sit and talk about someone I love for an hour. Because again, when in life do you do that?"

**B. Pause, not shut down (website)** · in 09:26, out 09:55 (~29s, stop on "but he couldn't")
> "I think you could probably just enjoy it… when people do speeches about you, it's always a bit like… But just the idea that you could listen to it and you're like, I can just genuinely accept this, because I don't have to feel weird about other people hearing it. It's just me in that moment hearing from that person. And again, it's the sort of thing that if I'd said to his face, he would have shut me down, but he couldn't."

**C. He's turned up more (/refer-griffiths "Why now")** · in 13:19, out 13:52 (~35s)
> "The fact that it's like Geoff and dad are going to go to the pub to talk about spirituality, I was like, that would never have happened. I couldn't see a world in which that would have happened had this not happened… I think it's stopped dad on autopilot, and I think he's making more conscious decisions in terms of family. He's turned up more."

**Backup D. Binged it like a box set** · in 04:47, out 05:10 (stop before "my children are playing")
> "Getting those chapters through was just so exciting… Geoff and I just binged it like it was a box set. Like a Netflix series. We could not ram it in our ears fast enough because it was just fascinating."

**Backup E. More than the thing** · in 29:13, out 29:55
> "I had focused on this idea of, it's a lovely record for the future. But actually, it's also like this shared journey that you go on with your family… you start it thinking it will just be one element, but it actually has all of these different springboards and payoffs."
