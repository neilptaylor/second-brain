## Addition to "MMOM Discovery Call — Script v2 (20 min)"

Insert into **§2 · WHAT BRINGS YOU HERE + THE TRIGGER**, after the existing trigger question, before moving to §3.

---

**"Have you looked into anything like this before — tried to write it down yourselves, or looked at another company?"**

*This is the Pitch OS "solution question" (has this been to anyone else for help), adapted. Two things it tells you:*

- *If they've tried and stalled (a half-written document, a voice memo project that never happened) — that's a live cost-of-inaction story for the proposal, and tells you the barrier was follow-through, not desire.*
- *If they've looked at another company — get the name if it's offered, don't fish for it. Tells you what they're comparing you against, and what they didn't like enough to walk away.*

Write down whatever they say — it goes in the proposal alongside the trigger.
