## 2. How many talks turn into a "yes": 35%

## 3. How long a "yes" takes: about 11 days
For the families who said yes, there were roughly 11 days between the first chat and the second. Some said yes within a week (Best in 5 days, Marsh in 6). The ones that dragged past a month mostly didn't happen, or took ages. Fast usually means yes.

From July to September I brought in £5,637. If every quarter looked like that, it adds up to £22,500 a year. The goal is £150,000. So I'm running at about a seventh of the speed I need.

## 5. New families this month: 0
None in August, none in September. The last two signed up on 23 and 24 July. That's the number that worries me most, and it's why everything else is slow.

The product works and the money's safe for now, but not enough people are walking through the door. To hit £150k I need about 8 first chats a month. I'm having 2.
