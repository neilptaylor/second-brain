```
At 17, he put a Halloween mask on his head and rode his brand new Aprilia round the village.

It slipped over his eyes.
He went into a hedge.

Then he went home and waited for the biggest telling off of his life.

A few weeks ago I got Daniel and his dad on a call to finish the audio documentary of his dad's life.

I asked his dad what it was like to listen to his son talk about him for an hour.

He said most of it he already knew.
Then he said he'd never heard the real reason Daniel came off that bike.

He thought it was really, really funny.

He still remembered the day Daniel came home.
"I don't think I've ever seen him so crestfallen."

Daniel said if he'd told him at the time, it would have been "napalm in the house for the next year."

His dad said:

"Maybe it didn't have to be 25 years of time."

Daniel said:

"That says more about how scared I am of you."

Both of them laughing.

I think most families have a hedge like that.
Something small we never said.
It got big because we never said it.

Repost if there's a Halloween mask in your family.

P.S. Daniel wrote about the whole experience last week, and told it better than I have. I got a bit emotional at the end of that call. I'd give a lot to hear what my own dad's hedge was.
```
