## About (paste into Details > Overview)

Nobody stopped loving each other. Life just got in the way.

We talk to our parents about the boiler, the weather, the dishwasher. Twelve minutes on the phone and none of it is the real stuff.

Me & My Old Man is a 12-week experience. I sit down with your parent, at their own pace, and record their life story in their own voice as an audio documentary. Your siblings are interviewed too, so you hear each other properly, often for the first time in years.

You end up with your parent's voice, permanent and shareable. You also end up with a family that feels closer than it did.

I started this after my dad died and I couldn't recall his voice. My son was born six days later. They never met. I didn't want that gap for anyone else's family.

37 families. 7 countries. 380+ hours of interviews.
