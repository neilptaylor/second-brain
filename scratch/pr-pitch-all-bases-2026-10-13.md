# PR pitch: all bases (core product + grief), after Katy Evans call

**Subject:** Twelve weeks, a parent's life story, and the children who sat down to hear it

Hi [name],

My dad died in March 2021. My son was born six days later, and I had never recorded my dad's voice. That is why I started Me & My Old Man.

I interview a parent over guided sessions, I interview their adult children too, and I edit it all into ten chapters of that parent's life story, in their own words. Then the family listens back together. 38 families, seven countries, 370+ hours so far. Most of these families aren't broken. They've drifted.

I'd like to offer you or one of your writers one of two ways in:

1. **Talk to a family who've been through it.** A parent and their adult children, who can tell you what they heard that they'd never heard before. [See the Family Documentary.](https://mmom-familydocumentary.netlify.app/)
2. **Go through the 12 weeks yourself**, with one of your own parents, on me. Write whatever you like about it.

One family's story is different. A woman took her case to the Court of Session in Edinburgh to have children with her late husband, and it made the [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2020/sep/15/scotland-woman-seeks-legal-approval-to-use-late-husbands-sperm-ivf) and the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-scotland-north-east-orkney-shetland-54294414). Last year she asked me to capture who he was. I interviewed his parents, his two brothers, three of his friends and her, and built an audio documentary with his laugh in it, and his silly guitar songs, so that when their two children ask "what was Dad like", there is an answer. [Here's how she felt about it.](https://www.meandmyoldman.co.uk/#stories-of-those-weve-lost) That one is yours to talk to her about, or to listen to and write about, in time for Grief Awareness Week, which starts on 2 December.

For gift lists and features, I can work to your deadlines for Christmas, Mother's Day and Father's Day. If either is a fit, I'd love to talk.

Neil
