ONE INTRODUCTION

**A Snapshot, on me**

An hour and a half with me, about your life as it is right now. I help you prepare, then I ask and you talk, and nobody interrupts. Within a week you get it back: about an hour of edited audio, plus short video clips.

You've just recorded where your family came from. This is where it is now. Take it a year on, as a new chapter of your documentary. Or give it to one of your children, so they don't wait until they're seventy-five to tell theirs.

Small print: the Snapshot reward expires 12 months after your family's booking.

# Cold-audience one-liners, options

1. The busiest years of our lives are the ones we remember least clearly. Snapshot is an hour and a half to tell yours while you're still in them: for you next month, for your children next decade.
2. You'll remember that this year happened. You won't remember what it felt like. Tell it now, once for you and once for the kids who'll one day ask.
3. Most of us know what our parents did at forty. Almost none of us know what it felt like. Snapshot means your children won't have the same gap.
4. The school run, the 2am worry, the thing your seven-year-old said on Tuesday. Too ordinary to write down, too precious to lose. An hour and a half to keep them.
5. One day your children will ask what you were like at their age, and at yours. Give them more than a shrug and a photo album.
6. Nobody records the middle of their life. It's the bit their children end up most wanting to hear.
