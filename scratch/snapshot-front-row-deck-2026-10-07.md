## Application form

1. First name, email
2. How do we know each other? (friend / we've worked together / I was on your podcast / other)
3. Where do your people gather? Tick any: newsletter, podcast, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp or school groups, a club or team, other
4. Roughly how many people would see it if you shared it once? (under 50 / 50 to 500 / 500 to 5,000 / 5,000+)
5. Would you be happy to share Snapshot with them once in the next month? (yes / maybe / no)
6. Who's it for: you, your partner, or turns?
7. Is there a parent or grandparent whose story you'd love to have on record? (yes, living / yes, no longer with us / not really)
8. **If you could sit down with one person from your family who's no longer here, who would it be, and what's the one question you'd ask them?**

Q8 is Steve's question. It does the selecting. A specific "my dad, what was he like at my age" answer is the main-offer buyer, so follow up later about the family documentary.

## The numbers

Cost per Snapshot is **£145** (V10: 1.5 session hours at £80, plus 1 edit hour at £25). It's your time, not cash. Video takes about another £25 of your time. The decade review is assumed at 3 ops hours, £75 [check]. The referral is a flat £50 per booking. The margin floor is 50%.

| Pack | Price | Per year | Cost | Margin | After £50 referral |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 year | £199 | £199 | £145 | 27.1% | £4 left, 2.0% |
| 3 years | £499 | £166 | £435 | 12.8% | £14, 2.8% |

At £199, a referred booking leaves you about £4. The referral would eat all of it.

Only the single clears the 50% floor. The packs trade margin for a customer you keep for years, which is Steve's whole point. The "referred people get a price they can't get elsewhere" link could be the 3-year at £749 or a one-off at £249. £249 less the £50 referral less £145 leaves **£54**.

### What the Front Row costs you

- 10 people × £99 × 5 years = **£4,950** in.
- 50 Snapshots × £145 = **£7,250** of your time.
