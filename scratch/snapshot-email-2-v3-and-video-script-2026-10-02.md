## Email 2

**Subject:** What Snapshot actually is
**Preview:** A couple of hours from you. A year you get to keep.

Hey,

Here's the shape of it, and exactly what I'd need from you.

**[VIDEO THUMBNAIL, linked: "Snapshot in 90 seconds"]**

**What you get**

The story of your year, in your own voice. An hour and a half where the phone goes face down and you check in on how this year has actually been.

An hour, fully produced, back to you within 7 days. The audio for the car or the dog walk, and the video version too, so the kids can see your face and not just hear it.

**What it takes from you**

- 30 minutes on a short form, so I know where to dig. Cuppa in hand.
- An hour and a half with me, online, wherever you can get quiet. The kitchen table once the kids are down. The car in the school car park.

That's it. I ask, you talk, nobody interrupts.

**The founding five**

Snapshot will be £300. The first five pay £99, with the video version on me.

In return, I'll ask for 30 more minutes once you've listened back: a 2-minute video of you saying what it was like, and 20 minutes of honest feedback on what worked and what didn't.

And I'll ask you to move quickly. Form back and session done within two weeks of saying yes, so I can learn fast and get it right for everyone who comes after you.

You help me get it right. You get it for a third of the price.

I'm closing the founding five on Friday 16 October, or sooner if they go.

If you want one, hit reply with "I'm in" and I'll send you two dates.

Neil

P.S. In 2012 I took a photo every day. It's by far the year I remember most. Where I'd love Snapshot to go: one every year, recorded the week before your birthday, back to you on the day. A birthday present to yourself.
