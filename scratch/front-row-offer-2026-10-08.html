The Front Row: offer, flow and pricing

Draft v2, 8 October 2026. Built from the Steve Wade call on 7 October and the Front Row deck. Numbers from pricing.md and Cost Model V10. Nothing here is live until you sign it off.

The name

The Front Row. Yes, that's the one you chose on 7 October. The people closest to the stage, who see it first and tell everyone else. It beats "Founding Fathers" because the group has women in it.

Where it sits

  • Founding Five: £99, one Snapshot, video included, feedback call. Closes Friday 16 October. They become the first five of the Front Row, and get the five-year lock offered at their feedback call.
  • The Front Row: the next ten places. £99 a year, locked for five years, in return for sharing it once with their people.
  • Everyone else: the public price.

The offer

Once a year, an hour and a half with you, online, about the year they've just lived. Within 7 days it comes back as the story of their year: about an hour, fully produced, in audio and video. Do it five years running and they can hear themselves change.

What they get

  • £99 a year, locked for five years. Everyone after them pays £299.
  • Video included every year. Just them, talking to camera, you cut out.
  • Share it with a partner: "this year's your turn".
  • £50 for everyone who books through their link.

What they give back

  • A two-minute video about what it was like, after the first one lands.
  • Share it once with their people: newsletter, podcast, LinkedIn, the WhatsApp group. You write it with them if they want.
  • Their people get their own link, at £249 for a single Snapshot. A price nobody else can get.

The flow

  • 1. Personal message from you. "There's an offer I can make you." Link to the unlisted page, e.g. /snapshot-fr7q. Same VSL as /snapshot.
  • 2. Application form. About three minutes. Ends with Steve's question: "If you could sit down with one person from your family who's no longer here, who would it be, and what would you ask them?"
  • 3. You pick. Reach first, then ICP signal (a parent's story they want on record), then a mix of women, men and couples.
  • 4. You message them yourself. Payment link, reflection form, booking link.
  • 5. Record. Within two weeks of the yes. Delivered within 7 days.
  • 6. Two-minute video once they've listened.
  • 7. The share. Within a month. Their own link, £249, £50 back to them per booking.
  • 8. Every year after. A reminder a month before their birthday week. Record, deliver.
  • 9. The quiet door. Anyone whose Q8 answer is "my dad, what was he like at my age" gets a conversation about the family documentary. Not on the page. In your own time.

The 3, 5 and 10 year packages

Cost per Snapshot is £145 of your time (1.5 session hours at £80, plus an hour of editing at £25). The decade review is assumed at 3 ops hours, £75. Margin floor is 50%.

Public, paid up front (recommended, the £299 ladder)

  • 1 year: £299. Margin 51.5%. After a £50 referral, £104 left.
  • 3 years: £749 (£250 a year). Margin 41.9%. After referral, £264.
  • 5 years: £1,095 (£219 a year). Margin 33.8%. After referral, £320.
  • 10 years, with a decade review: £1,950 (£195 a year). Margin 21.8%. After referral, £375.

Only the single clears 50%. The packs trade margin for a customer you keep for years, which is Steve's whole point. On Steve's £199 one-off, a 5-year pack loses £26 once you've paid a referral, so the £199 ladder can't carry the £50.

Front Row

  • 3 years: £99 a year, £297. The minimum term, per Steve's two-to-three-year floor.
  • 5 years: £99 a year, £495. The headline deal.
  • 10 years: £99 a year for years one to five, then £199 a year for six to ten, decade review included. £1,490 in total, still well under the public £1,950.

Over the 3, 5 and 10 year terms, your time comes to £510, £850 and £1,775 against them.

Recommend paid yearly, not up front. It keeps the £99 feeling small and gives you a natural renewal moment each year.

What it costs you

Live numbers: V10, Front Row tab. It pulls the Snapshot cost, your hourly rates and the margin floor straight from Bolt-Ons, Controls and Pricing Grid, so V10 stays the source of truth. The yellow cells are the calls you still need to make. The figures below are what it showed on 8 October.

  • With video included, a Front Row Snapshot costs £170 of your time, not £145.
  • 10 people × £99 × 5 years = £4,950 in, against £8,500 of your time.
  • Gap: £3,550, or £355 a person over five years. That's the marketing spend.
  • On a £249 referred-friend link, each booking leaves you £54. So each person needs about 6.6 referred bookings to pay back their gap. At £299 it's about 3.4.
  • Time: about 35 hours a year, before referrals.

The honest read: £99 a year with video in only makes sense if the share actually brings in people. Two levers fix it fastest: put the referred-friend link at £299, or make the Front Row price £149 a year.
