# Daniel Beard, LinkedIn testimonial post (draft in Daniel's voice)

Modelled on the Ed Finley, Steve Millard and Angus Watts posts in the Testimonials doc. Sources: Beard Wrap Up (17 Sept 2026), Daniel's Ch1 session (25 Feb 2026), Beard clip brief.

```
I did something this year that I'll never regret.

The day before my first session with Neil Taylor, I went to the funeral of my best mate's dad.

He was 85.
A great life, a family who loved him.
And a eulogy that was all about what he'd done for other people.

I came home thinking about my own dad.

Not in a sad way.
In a "how much do I actually know?" way.

Because for most of my life, all I really knew about him was motorbikes and work.

I'd seen Neil's posts about losing his own dad, and the business he started afterwards, Me & My Old Man.

Neil interviews you for an hour about your dad.
Then he sits down with your dad for hours and turns it into an audio documentary.

My dad was apprehensive to start with. He'll tell you that himself.

Then he started talking.

His dad and six brothers, walking four miles to volunteer the day after war was declared.
A cooling problem on a Formula One car that he solved, and a mate at McLaren tells me they still use it today.
And the real reason I came off my first motorbike at 17.

(It involves a Halloween mask. Dad found that one very funny.)

I'll be honest about my own hour.

I was embarrassed to listen back to it.
I'd gone in with all my questions about his strictness and how he brought us up, and it felt like an outsourced therapy session.
I sounded like a petulant teenager having a go at his dad over a missed rugby match.

Then I listened to eight or nine hours of him.

And something shifted.

He's shaped by what he went through.
I'm shaped by what I went through.

A son just wants to understand his dad.

Now I do.

On our final call I said I was looking forward to the next couple of chapters over the next two decades.

Dad said: "You don't look forward to it as much as I do."

And one day my grandchildren will hear their grandfather tell it himself.

If there's a parent in your life you only half know, talk to Neil. He does a free half hour chat: neil@meandmyoldman.co.uk or www.meandmyoldman.co.uk

My dad, who was apprehensive to begin with, is happy to tell you about it too.
```
