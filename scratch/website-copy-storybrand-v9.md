## CHANGES LOG, v8 → v9

**Hero H2.** v8's H2 ("You know the role they play. You've just never had the space to hear their whole story.") was all feeling and no product. Feedback from a marketing contact: she had to search the site to work out what was being sold. New H2 is the compressed version of Section 15 ("what you get, what you really get"). The old H2's first half is folded into the success glimpse so the line isn't lost.

**Bridge line after the flagship video.** The video is Neil's story (Patrick, the Monday and the Sunday). Section 3 is the visitor's story (Sunday lunches, twelve-minute phone calls). This line hands the story from one to the other, which is the guide-to-hero turn the framework wants. It sits outside the hero so the hero stays about the visitor.

**Open:** "12-week" is Silver. Bronze is 4 weeks and Gold 16. Fine if Silver is the lead offer, but check it doesn't clash with Section 9.
**Families:** 35 → 38 everywhere on the site, from the Total Hours sheet (5 Oct 2026). The sheet also shows 396 hours (site says 350+) and more countries than 7. Not changed, Neil to confirm.
