# Nicola Griffiths: 2-minute testimonial edit

Source: `nicola-neil (1).txt` (Riverside testimonial session, ~41 min). Same build as Geoff's: one stitched film, hook first, captions burned in, ends on the change in Dad.
Times are where Nicola's turn starts. "~" means partway through the turn.

Hard rules for this cut: nothing involving her children (no pool, no Toby, no "played it to my kids", no Wales holiday). No heart attack. Nothing that leans on knowing Neil.

---

**1. Hook: the hour** (~03:35)
"I don't think I knew how much it would mean to just sit and talk about someone I love for an hour. Because again, when in life do you do that? Like it was so lovely."

**2. How it started** (00:40)
"I obviously came about the whole process by my brother pitching it to me, which is unusual for him to bring something heartfelt. Normally the joke is that I think of all the heartfelt things and he just throws money at things. So for him to turn around and be like, I've got this great idea for Father's Day, I was like, whoa."

**3. Someone else asking** (02:36)
"I was hopeful that it would kind of bring information that we hadn't come across before, or that we hadn't thought to ask, or that we hadn't felt was our place to ask." [cut] (~03:15) "There's a joke, isn't there, that everyone's better at talking about deep subjects in a car because they're not facing each other."

**4. Desert Island Discs** (05:43)
"It was like listening to my dad on an elongated Desert Island Discs, which was really nice."

**5. Dad hears it** (06:12)
"He came round to my house... he was clearly really touched by it." [cut] (07:00) "I think he just hadn't seen himself through our eyes, if that makes sense."

**6. The other way round** (08:34)
"We think about it as like, we're hearing all this amazing family information. And we never think, he gets to hear how important he is to us, which I just thought was lovely."

**7. The pub** (~13:00)
"The fact that it's like, Jeff and Dad are going to go to the pub to talk about spirituality. I was like, that would never have happened. I couldn't see a world in which that would have happened had this not happened."

**8. Close: Dad turned up** (~13:35)
"I think it's stopped Dad on autopilot, and I think he's making more conscious decisions in terms of family, which is really, really nice. He's turned up more." [cut] (~14:00) "Since we started this process, he's just prioritised family more."

Reserve, if it runs short: (~29:40) "You start it thinking it will just be one element, but it actually has all of these different springboards and payoffs."
