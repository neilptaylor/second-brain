# LinkedIn A: the open question

I have received this line twice this week from sons and daughters in their forties.

"I'd love to. Money's just tight right now."

And it is. School fees, the mortgage, the boiler, holidays already cut back.

But something's been nagging me.

If your parents rang tomorrow and said "we've got two tickets to the World Cup final, they're yours", you'd say yes before they'd finished the sentence.

If they said "we're taking the whole family away for a weekend, our treat", you'd be looking for your passport.

You'd take either. Gladly. No guilt.

Now say this to the same two people, in the same kitchen:

"Mum, Dad. I'd like to do something for us. I'd like you to pay for it."

Same parents.
Same money.
Same love.

Completely different sentence.

I don't have the answer.

I'm curious what stops us.

Repost if you've felt that in your own throat.
