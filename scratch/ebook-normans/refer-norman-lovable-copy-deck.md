## 2. What it is

**Kicker:** What it is

**H2:** Your mum or dad's stories. In their voice. To keep.

**Para 1 (fixed):** There's a version of your own mum or dad you've never fully met. This is how you meet them, while they're still here to tell it. Not the headline facts. The texture underneath: the Saturday mornings with a grandfather, the girl met years later by a chair in a church, the wage negotiation that took a year and changed how a whole factory was run.

**Para 2:** Six to sixteen hours of recorded conversation, led personally by Neil Taylor. Shaped into twelve chapters for the Normans, with each of the three children reflecting back and a final hour with everyone together. Delivered as an audio documentary the whole family keeps, in David's own voice, over the old photographs, and passes on. Most projects run 4 to 16 weeks start to finish.
