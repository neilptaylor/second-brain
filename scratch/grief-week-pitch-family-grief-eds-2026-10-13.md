# Pitch: family features and grief editors, National Grief Awareness Week (2 Dec)

Built from Katy Evans' advice on the 9 Oct call: hang it on a date, offer two things (me, or a family), keep the court case as background not the headline, no links needed.

---

**Subject:** For Grief Awareness Week: the dad his children will never meet

Hi [name],

Ahead of National Grief Awareness Week on 2 December, I have a story I think your readers would feel.

A few years ago a woman went to the Court of Session in Edinburgh to win the right to have children with her late husband. She won, and they now have two young children. Last year she asked me to capture who he was. I interviewed his parents, his two brothers, three of his friends and her, and built an audio documentary of him, with recordings of his laugh and his silly guitar songs, so when the children ask "what was Dad like?", there's an answer.

I run Me & My Old Man. I started it after my dad died in 2021, six days before my son was born, without me ever having recorded his voice. Most families come to me to capture a parent's story while they're still here. Some come to me after.

Two things I can offer: an interview with me, or an introduction to her. She's happy to talk.

Cheers,
Neil Taylor
Me & My Old Man | meandmyoldman.co.uk
