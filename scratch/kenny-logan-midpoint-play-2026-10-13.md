# Kenny Logan → Gabby Logan → The Mid•Point

Researched 13 Oct 2026. Not confirmed by today's searches, from general knowledge, check before using: Kenny's 70 caps and Wasps, twins born 2005, Gabby's 1990 Commonwealth Games, Daniel collapsing in the garden with Terry, Christine being alive, Mid•Point starting in 2020.

## Kenny Logan

- Kenneth McKerrow Logan, born Stirling 1972. Rugby, not football: Scotland wing, 70 caps, Wasps in the Premiership.
- Grew up in a farming family near Stirling and still farms there. Severe dyslexia, left Wallace High School without sitting exams. Gabby pushed him into the Dore programme in his thirties and he now champions it.
- Prostate cancer, diagnosed Feb 2022 with no symptoms. He got checked because of a Mid•Point episode on men's hormones. Had the prostate removed. Now a loud voice for testing, and he's been on Mid•Point twice about it ("The Prostate Episode" and "Prostate Cancer: The Follow Up").
- Talks openly: he said he cried the night before the op because he didn't want to leave the kids or Gabby.
- Twins Lois and Reuben (born 2005). Reuben plays pro rugby.
- His parents: not found. Nothing published names them or says whether they're alive. Ask around before you write anything about them.

## Gabby Logan

- Born Gabrielle Yorath, Leeds, 1973. Ex Wales rhythmic gymnast (1990 Commonwealth Games). BBC presenter: Match of the Day, athletics, Six Nations.
- Dad: Terry Yorath, Leeds United and Wales midfielder, later Wales manager. Died 7 Jan 2026 in Leeds, aged 75, after a short illness. The call came through while she was presenting Match of the Day live and she left mid-show, Mark Chapman took over. She called him a warrior on the pitch and a kind-hearted, generous man with a wicked sense of humour off it. Months later she told The Times she was "still processing it".
- Mum: Christine Yorath, alive as far as reporting shows.
- Brother Daniel died in 1992 at 15, from an undiagnosed heart condition, while kicking a ball about with Terry in the garden. Siblings Louise and Jordan.
- She has talked grief elsewhere (On The Mend podcast, about Daniel). I found no Mid•Point episode on Terry's death yet.

## The Mid•Point with Gabby Logan

- Running since 2020, on Acast. Midlife: health, menopause, careers, relationships, reinvention. Guests are mostly well known (Sophie Raworth, Frida Redknapp, Jenni Falconer, Matt Hampson, Mark Foster).
- Still running: new episodes week of 13 Oct 2026, now listed as "Life at the Midpoint with Gabby Logan" (Neil confirmed).
- Fit: strong. Her audience is the sandwich generation, and she's now a daughter nine months on from losing her dad, plus a mum still with her own.

## The play

Two prizes, one door. Gabby is the bigger name, Kenny is the way in.

2. **The Mid•Point.** Still running, new episodes this week ([Apple](https://podcasts.apple.com/gb/podcast/life-at-the-midpoint-with-gabby-logan/id1527545442), now titled "Life at the Midpoint"). If Kenny gets it, he's the one who tells Gabby.
