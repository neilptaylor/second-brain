## 1. Pain Points — from 26056267
**Hook:** He'd already decided. He just didn't know how to tell her.

He'd read everything. Asked every practical question. Ready to book.

Then he went quiet: "I just don't know how I'm going to tell her."

Not because he thought she'd say no. Because he was already rehearsing it going wrong.

Here's what I told him. It's not about persuading them. It's about telling them the truth of why you want it.

Not "I've booked something."

"I want to hear you tell it, in your own words, while I still can."

## 3. Transformation — from 25864582
**Hook:** His granddad was just... his granddad. Nice enough. A bit distant.
